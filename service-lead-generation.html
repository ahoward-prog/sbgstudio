What We Do

Lead Generation

Lead generation has fundamentally changed. The old playbook of interruption marketing no longer works in a world where buyers control the information flow. Here’s what actually drives qualified leads today.

Our Approach

Understanding the Modern Buyer Journey

Buyers complete 70% of their research before ever talking to sales.

The buyer journey today looks nothing like it did five years ago. Buyers read reviews, compare alternatives, and form opinions long before filling out a contact form. This creates a challenge: how do you influence decisions you can’t see happening?

The answer is being present at every stage with content that actually helps, not content that sells — educational content at awareness, comparison content at consideration, proof content at decision.

The Sales Pipeline Reality

Better 50 leads that convert at 20% than 500 that convert at 2%.

Most companies confuse lead volume with pipeline health. They celebrate hitting lead targets while their sales teams struggle with unqualified prospects who were never going to buy.

We focus on pipeline quality over lead quantity: being ruthless about qualification, transparent about pricing and fit, and willing to disqualify prospects early rather than waste everyone’s time.

Case Study

Transcom

A global customer experience specialist providing customer care, sales, technical support, and credit management services — 29,000 work-at-home specialists at 50 contact centers across 21 countries, in 33 languages. The challenge: build a lead generation campaign that promotes brand awareness through multiple lead magnets to drive marketing qualified leads (MQLs) and increase the sales pipeline.

Top of Funnel

  • → LinkedIn employee amplification and sponsored social content
  • → Organic social content on Facebook
  • → Paid search on targeted key phrases

Mid-Funnel

  • → Facebook & LinkedIn native lead gen ads
  • → LinkedIn targeted audience & retargeting
  • → Facebook retargeting
  • → Google retargeting to HubSpot landing page

Bottom of Funnel

  • → Sales team connections on LinkedIn
  • → Email nurture drip campaign with call to action

“This project reinforced something we see repeatedly: lead generation success depends on alignment between marketing and sales. We spent as much time on the handoff process as we did on the campaigns themselves — clear lead scoring criteria, agreed-upon qualification standards, and regular feedback loops turned a lead generation program into a revenue generation program.”
