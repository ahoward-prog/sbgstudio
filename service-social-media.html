What We Do

Social Media

Most companies treat social media as a megaphone. Post content, hope for engagement, wonder why it doesn’t work. Social media isn’t about broadcasting — it’s about conversation. The brands that succeed understand this distinction.

Our Approach

What Most Companies Get Wrong

Posting more doesn’t increase engagement. Posting content that sparks conversation does.

Social media management becomes a full-time job when companies confuse activity with strategy. They post daily because they think consistency matters. It doesn’t — relevance matters.

We’ve managed social for companies from startups to major brands. Most companies optimize for the wrong metric; the companies that succeed prioritize quality of conversation over quantity of posts.

Platform Strategy vs Platform Presence

Better to dominate one platform than be invisible on five.

Most companies are on every platform because they think they should be. This dilutes effort and produces mediocre results everywhere. The question isn’t “should we be on Instagram?” It’s “where does our audience actually engage?”

B2B companies waste resources on Instagram while ignoring LinkedIn. Consumer brands chase TikTok trends their customers don’t care about. Strategy means choosing where to compete, not trying to be everywhere.

Case Study

GOOD DAY FARM

The South’s #1 medical dispensary cannabis brand, with a loyal following across its online and retail network in Arkansas, Missouri, Mississippi, and Louisiana, built on an approachable brand and a high-tech, high-touch combination of cultivation and distribution.

Objective

GOOD DAY FARM started with a single store and asked us to build their brand with a heavy social media presence from the ground up — multiple websites, social media, text and email campaigns, and a new database resource customized for the cannabis industry.

Tactics

A systematic process of content creation, scheduling, and posting; weekly calls with leadership and store managers; a content calendar with approval tracking in Monday.com; and integrated text/email marketing via Alpine IQ for each new location.

Results

Within the first few weeks we established a cadence of messaging that resonated with customers, helping the first four stores hit their sales goals, then facilitated four more openings plus two retail outlets with distinct branding.
