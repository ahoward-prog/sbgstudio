What is Geofencing?

A smart way to connect, engaging people exactly where and when it matters most.

Imagine setting up an invisible digital fence around your favorite spots on a map. Whenever someone steps in or out of this zone with their smartphone’s location services turned on, it kicks off a series of actions—personalized ads, alerts, or other cool stuff tailored just for them. It’s a smart way to connect, engaging people exactly where and when it matters most.

Where to Use Geofencing:

Local Ads for Businesses

For small businesses, targeting ads locally means you can entice nearby customers with offers when they’re most likely to stop by, whether it’s a lunch special at your cafe or a flash sale at your boutique.

Competitor Targeting

Use geo-targeting to turn competitors into opportunities. Send a tempting offer to someone’s device right when they’re at your rival’s location—it’s a strategic nudge that could swing them your way.

Combining Tactics

Amp up your geo-targeting by mixing it with demographic or custom audience filters. Imagine you want to target ads only to women over 40 in a specific area, or you have a list of customers you only want to reach when they’re close by—geo-targeting makes this seamless.

B2B Marketing

Big deals need precise targeting. Geofencing targets ads to the corporate headquarters of a potential business client, or tailor your messages to reach multiple companies on your prospect list directly at their offices.

Event Targeting

Miss out on a booth at a big conference? No problem. Geo-target the event location to ensure your ads reach the attendees, making your presence felt without being there physically.

Industries that Benefit from Geofencing

Retail

Retailers can use geofencing to send promotional notifications or discount offers to customers when they are near a store. This encourages foot traffic and can directly increase sales.

Automotive

Car dealerships utilize geofencing to target potential customers with special deals when they are near a dealership. This can also be effective for service reminders when past customers are nearby.

Hospitality and Tourism

Hotels and tourist attractions use geofencing to offer welcome messages, deals on upgrades, or information about amenities as guests arrive. Tourist attractions can promote exhibits or special discounts as visitors approach.

Health and Fitness

Gyms and health clubs can target promotions for membership or classes to people who frequently visit nearby areas, or perhaps are near a competitor’s location.

Real Estate

Agents can trigger property alerts to potential buyers when they enter a specific neighborhood or pass near a property for sale, providing timely and relevant information that can aid in the buying process.

Events and Entertainment

For concerts, theaters, and sports events, geofencing can promote ticket sales and special events to individuals within a certain vicinity of the venue or inform them about upcoming events they might be interested in.

Food and Beverage

Restaurants and bars can attract more customers by sending push notifications about daily specials or happy hour deals to people within a nearby area. This tactic can effectively increase immediate foot traffic.

Education

Educational institutions, especially colleges and universities, can use geofencing to target prospective students with information about open days, campus tours, or special programs when they are nearby.
