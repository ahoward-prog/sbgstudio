What We Do

UI/UX Design

UI/UX design is built to enhance the user journey. It anticipates questions and enables users to resolve them at every step. It prizes clarity, consistency, and elegance — the absolute difference between product success or failure.

Our Approach

UI/UX Design is Interconnected

Users don’t separate UI from UX — they just know if something works.

UI and UX are two different disciplines that are critically dependent on each other. A beautiful design will not succeed if an app or website is confusing to navigate from one screen to the next, and the easy joy of a well-conceived app will miss the mark if the visual interface isn’t attractive.

Foundation of Human-Centered Design

Success depends on research, not just available analytics.

A digital and mobile-first world demands thoughtful, intuitive, unique solutions grounded in business objectives. Insights must be built from a competitive landscape and grounded by real user personas — understanding user behavior means observing how people actually use products, not how we hope they’ll use them.

Our UI/UX Process

An iterative design thinking methodology that aligns product requirements and strategy with the needs of the user, to create a solution that’s intuitive, efficient, and simple to navigate.

Case Study

NICE User Group App

NICE (NASDAQ: NICE) is the worldwide leader in cloud-based enterprise software, used by over 25,000 organizations in more than 190 countries, including over 85 of the Fortune 100.

Objective

The NICE User Group (NUG) is a global knowledge community of more than 11,000 members. NICE leadership asked us to redesign a cumbersome desktop interface for mobile delivery, to boost acceptance beyond a modest 1,500 log-ins per month.

Outcome

We worked with the NUG team and internal programmer to synthesize user feedback and design an intuitive app for mobile and desktop delivery in an aggressive 8-week timeframe, delivering Adobe XD files ready for their developers. The new app has been well-received, with steadily increasing participation.
