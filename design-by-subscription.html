FAQs

How is a subscription from SpringBolt Studio different from an agency retainer?

An agency retainer is typically based on a number of agency hours dedicated to your account per month. With a subscription, you don’t have to track hours. You can submit as many requests and revisions as you like.

How do I know I will receive quality design work?

SpringBolt Studio has been helping marketers succeed for 30 years and every assignment is reviewed by a Creative Director.

Why do you request that I only submit one request or one revision at a time?

The subscription model is a more streamlined version of the traditional agency model. As such, designers need to work more efficiently and being allowed to focus on one assignment at a time makes this possible.

What is if I need more than what is available through your subscriptions?

No problem. Just give us a call and we can discuss a more customized approach to your needs.

Does your company believe that the traditional agency model is dead?

Not at all. However, we also understand that customers are generally better served when they have more choices. This was the inspiration for offering design subscriptions.
