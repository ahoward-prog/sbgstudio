Do It Yourself Marketing Versus Go-to-Market Accelerator

We get it. Your startup isn’t ready to hire a full-time marketing expert or big advertising agency yet. But that doesn’t mean you have to get by or just do the marketing yourself. In fact, there are big risks in taking this approach that can create some big problems down the road.

Do It Yourself Marketing

  • — Funding and resources are not managed well leading to missed opportunities.
  • — Brand messaging is inconsistent, confusing, or ineffective and reduces impact.
  • — Measurement of sales and marketing efforts is non-existent, making it difficult to track ROI.
  • — Learning happens slowly as you inevitably make mistakes and delay your success.
  • — Investors may get nervous about your lack of a clear go-to-market strategy.
  • — Speed-to-market is hindered by indecision and lack of expertise leads to costly mistakes.

Go-to-Market Accelerator

  • ✓ Funding and resources management plan is clearly defined up-front.
  • ✓ Brand messaging is consistent, impactful, and based on market analysis.
  • ✓ Measurement based on clear objectives and tactics are optimized as you move forward.
  • ✓ Learning is accelerated as you let a seasoned marketing experts do all the heavy lifting.
  • ✓ Investors are impressed by the expertise and thoughtfulness behind your plan.
  • ✓ Speed-to-market is baked into the solution, including quickly pivoting when necessary.
