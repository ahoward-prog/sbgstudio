Brand Strategy

A brand is a unique asset that reflects who a company is and what it can do. Here’s how we work with our teams to make it powerful and create perceived value and impact.

Our Approach

Building Brands That Generate Revenue

Successful brands are built on three things AI cannot replicate.

Over time, we’ve learned that successful brands are built on three things AI cannot replicate: deep client relationships, industry-specific expertise, and the ability to see opportunities others miss. These are human advantages that matter more than ever.

We guide our teams in building durable, long-term relationships with our clients, with many lasting decades. We act as a robust network of resources, referrals, and strategic partners who understand their business as deeply as they do.

Strategy-Driven Creativity

Great brands emerge when strategy and creativity work in tandem.

We’ve learned that great brands emerge when strategy and creativity work in tandem. In an environment where AI can generate infinite creative variations, strategic clarity becomes the differentiator.

When we work with companies, we start by immersing ourselves in their business. We study their competitive landscape, understand their market position, and identify what makes them genuinely different. This strategic foundation determines which creative paths will drive results and which will waste resources.

Case Study

Love Envelopes

A leading custom envelope manufacturer and key player in the $1.5 trillion U.S. mailing industry. Founded more than 60 years ago with plants in Dallas and Tulsa, the printing facility manufactures nearly three billion envelopes every year.

Objective

While demand for traditional transactional mailings was declining, segments like direct marketing, greeting cards, and event announcements held steady. We helped Love Envelopes solidify their value proposition, get credit for significant investments in new equipment, and seize a bigger share of a gradually declining market.

Strategy

We planned a wide-ranging discovery process, talking with customers, sales reps, and employees across the business, then crafted a brand development workshop that generated a detailed creative briefing. Our positioning leveraged the company name and “Passion to Perform,” with a bold new red, black, and white palette.

Tactics

We developed a fresh corporate identity, a comprehensive style guide, and a new website, along with a full photo & video shoot. We also rebuilt the company’s e-commerce site at Letter Jacket Envelopes, improving the user experience and reducing manual order processing.
